Bring the receipts

"Receipts" means proof. Capstack turns the paper trail every owner already has (POS slips, transfer alerts, sales notebooks) into one stamped number with every reason listed. The seller says one thing, the buyer another: Capstack brings the receipts. Two ways to dress it below, both image-led, both on Capstack's soft gradients.

It printsEvery answer in onboarding adds a line to your receipt.
It stacksEach document you add slides onto your pile: Capstack.
It gets stampedWhen the valuation is ready, the seal lands.
It tears offThe report is the receipt you hand to a buyer or bank.
