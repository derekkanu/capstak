# Capstack brand kickoff (brand-process, started 2026-11-26)

## What exists (portfolio case study, ~/Derek Portfolio/public/projects/capstack)
- Wordmark "Capstack": heavy geometric sans, ligature-like s/p, with a bright green up-left arrow after the k.
  Monogram "Ck" + arrow. The arrow alone works as a symbol (mugs, sticker).
- Palette: Deep Growth #042E1B, Luminous Edge #D4ED6F (lime), Core Momentum #25D366 (green), Ember Light #FFF1E2,
  Obsidian Green #02151C, Radiant Orchid #FAB7EC, Aqua Drift #97F5E5, Soft Slate #F4F5F4, white.
- Type: Playfair Display italic (display), Satoshi Variable (body).
- Icons: rounded-square filled glyphs. Imagery: real African makers/owners at work; a doodle-line character on a
  billboard; small hand-drawn squiggles/stars on merch.
- Positioning line: "professional, modern, built for the African entrepreneur".
- Live site (capstak.vercel.app): cleaner and more modern (serif headline, sage/forest, lime), but no theme/story.

## Known answers (from the product conversation)
- Business: guided valuation for African SMEs, plain language, phone-first; tiers free / premium / brokers;
  marketplace later. See docs/capstack-spec.html.
- Feeling (Derek): **corporate but casual**: the in-between. Logo mixes a playful marketplace (Shopify) feel with
  a serious investor-money feel.
- Wants: keep what exists, make it cleaner and more modern (like the website), give it an essence/theme/story
  concept like Battle Grid's, maybe clean up typography.

## Answers (filled as we go)
1. **As a person:** a 31-year-old broker with loads of experience who actually knows what he's doing. Not fully
   corporate in dress, but enough to read as serious, and comfortable to be around. Very witty, strict on business.
   The perfect salesman + financial advisor in one. (Note: sharp, young, confident; wit carries the casual side,
   rigour carries the corporate side.)
2. **Never:** uptight, stiff, judgmental, complex, unreliable, passive/lazy ("lousy"; he's action-oriented).
   "Stylish" also came up: read as style for its own sake / showing off (confirm).
3. **References:** none in mind; likes the modernness of Deel (deel.com, confirmed): big confident sans headlines,
   lots of white, real people photographed with floating product chips ("Payroll complete $360,450", "Approved"),
   clean cards, one brand colour doing the work, product-first.
